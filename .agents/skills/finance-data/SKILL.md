# Finance-Data Skill

## Core Mandate
1. **Free Public Sources & Licences**:
   - **FEMA National Risk Index (NRI v1.19)**: US County-level Hurricane (`HRCN`) and Earthquake (`ERQK`) Expected Annual Loss and Risk Ratings. Licence: **Public Domain (17 U.S.C. § 105)**.
   - **USGS 2023 National Seismic Hazard Model (NSHM)**: Peak Ground Acceleration (PGA, 2% probability of exceedance in 50 years). Licence: **Public Domain (US Government Work)**.
   - **NOAA NHC HURDAT2**: Atlantic & Gulf tropical cyclone best-track database (1851–present). Licence: **Public Domain (US Government Work)**.
   - **Epoch AI Frontier Data Centers Hub**: Geolocation and MW capacity of hyperscale AI clusters. Licence: **CC BY 4.0**.
   - **PNNL IM3 Open Source Data Center Atlas**: US county/point data center inventory. Licence: **ODbL v1.0 / CC BY 4.0**.
   - **US EIA Form EIA-860**: Annual Electric Generator Report for power plant and substation locations/capacities. Licence: **Public Domain (US DOE)**.
   - **SEC EDGAR Form 10-K (Item 2 Properties)**: Corporate headquarters, wafer fabs, and regional campuses. Licence: **Public Domain (US SEC)**.
2. **No Paid Vendor Data or API Keys**: All reference checks must rely strictly on open public releases.
